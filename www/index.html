Agentic First

Company-first agentic creation.

Agentic First is a map for making company information and agent tools usable by agents. Open Company Information is the separate publishing platform: companies put their facts online in a structured, agent-readable form. The Open Tool Directory tracks the tools, standards, runtimes, protocols, and experiments that make agentic work possible.
